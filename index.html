How do you play?

It's as easy as 1, 2, 3, and you can play it on your own, or together with a grown-up!

👀

1. Look!

Every scene is a big, cosy Christmas world. Slide your finger to explore, and pinch to zoom in close.

👆

2. Tap!

See something on the list? Hunt for it, then tap it when you spot it hiding.

⭐

3. Hooray!

Find all 12 treasures to finish the scene, then pick the next adventure!

One game, two ways to play

Pick the mode that fits you. You can switch any time with one tap.

🧝 Little Elves

Ages 3 to 6
  • Bigger things that are easier to spot and tap
  • A friendly voice says the name of everything you find
  • Gentle, glowing hints whenever you want them
  • No timer, no rush, no pressure

🎯 Classic

Ages 6 to 8, and big kids too
  • Smaller, trickier hiding spots
  • A timer, so you can try to beat your best time
  • Three hints in every scene
  • Fun for the whole family
